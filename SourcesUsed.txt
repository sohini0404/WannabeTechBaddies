The flood severity levels in the application are determined using a hydrological risk assessment model that synthesizes data patterns and benchmarks from official Indian disaster management and meteorological authorities:


1. Primary Data Sources & Benchmarks
Central Water Commission (CWC) River Telemetry Guidelines:
River gauge flood thresholds (Low, Warning Level, Danger Level, Highest Flood Level / HFL).
Inflow and outflow dynamics across major river basins (Brahmaputra, Barak, Ganga, Godavari, Krishna, Mahanadi, Narmada, Yamuna, etc.) and major barrages (e.g., Dowleswaram, Prakasam, Farakka).
Embankment breach histories and historical vulnerable flood points (e.g., Bethukandi dyke in Cachar/Silchar, Budameru diversion channel in Vijayawada, Bharalu channel in Guwahati).

Official emergency dispatch contacts (NDRF 1078, SDMA 1070, DDMA 1077, ERSS 112).
